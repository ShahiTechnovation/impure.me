02 / BEYOND ENGINEERING

Code meets
community.

I founded Builder Base to connect developers with the tools, people, and opportunities they need to build.

That means sitting beside someone debugging their first deployment, preparing a workshop that starts with working code, and creating places where builders can find each other.

My Developer Relations and ecosystem work brings together technical workshops, hackathon programs, developer documentation and community distribution across India. Engineering gives me something useful to share; community helps me understand what to build next.
